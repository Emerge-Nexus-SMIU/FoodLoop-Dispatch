# Pilot and commercial roadmap

P0 and account/team/reporting hardening are implemented; see PROGRESS.md. Commercial readiness remains gated by RELEASE_READINESS.md.

## Controlled Pakistan pilot preparation

Confirm registered entity, support ownership, partner eligibility and handling policy. Approve privacy, terms, retention and licensing. Configure a real sender and demonstrate account emails arrive. Provision isolated production data, restricted runtime roles, administrator MFA, HTTPS and secret management; disable all demo identities. Activate monitoring and encrypted offsite backups; rehearse restoration and incident response. Complete manual accessibility/security review and representative load testing before public launch.

## Measured partner pilot

Onboard a small verified network, train operators and rehearse complete handovers. Measure acceptance latency, failed pickups, confirmed quantities by unit, mail failures and support cost. Existing invitations/inbox/CSV/policy controls support this phase. Validate current capacity assumptions and bounded directory/team limits before expanding. Consider recurring availability, rolling capacity allocation and routing integrations against measured demand.

## Commercial validation and extensions

Validate willingness to pay and operating cost with actual partners. Source pricing hypotheses are unvalidated; no live billing until separately authorized. Multi-organization switching, native mobile, advanced route optimization and integrations remain future work. Emissions reporting requires a defensible methodology, actual weights and regional factors; never invent conversions or combine meals/kg/items.
